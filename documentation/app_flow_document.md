# RasproView AI App Flow Document

## Onboarding and Sign-In/Sign-Up
When a new user arrives at the RasproView AI application, they first land on a welcoming page that briefly explains the platform’s capabilities for both recruiters and candidates. From there, the user can choose to sign up or sign in. Signing up requires providing an email address and creating a password. Recruiters and candidates select their role during sign-up so the system can direct them to the right dashboard. Once the form is completed, the user receives a confirmation email and clicks a link to verify their account.

If a recruiter invites a candidate directly, the candidate receives an email with a magic link that automatically creates and verifies their account when clicked. On the sign-in page, users enter their email and password to access the system. If they have forgotten their password, they click the “Forgot Password” link, enter their email, and receive a password reset link. After resetting their password, they are redirected to the sign-in page. Signing out is always available in the app sidebar or header; clicking “Sign Out” ends the session and returns the user to the sign-in page.

## Main Dashboard or Home Page
After signing in, the user lands on the main dashboard, which shares a consistent header and a sidebar navigation. The header displays the user’s name, a theme toggle for light or dark mode, and a profile menu. The sidebar lists links such as Dashboard, Interviews, Settings, and Sign Out. Recruiters see a summary of upcoming interviews, recent candidate submissions, and quick links to create a new interview. Candidates see pending interview invitations, a progress indicator for any active interview, and a button to begin an invited session.

From this dashboard, users can click the Interviews link to view a full list of interviews. The design uses responsive cards and tables built with the shadcn/ui library and styled by Tailwind CSS. Navigating between dashboard sections is as simple as clicking the corresponding sidebar item, which loads the new page without a full browser refresh.

## Detailed Feature Flows and Page Transitions

### Interview Creation Flow for Recruiters
A recruiter clicks “Interviews” in the sidebar and arrives at the Interviews page, where they see a list of existing interview templates. The recruiter then clicks “Create New Interview,” which opens a form. In that form, the recruiter enters an interview title, adds questions one by one, and sets any time restrictions. Next, they add candidate emails to send invitations. When they submit the form, the application saves the interview data to the database and triggers an email invitation to each candidate. After creation, the recruiter is redirected back to the Interviews list and sees the new interview in draft status.

### Candidate Interview Flow
When a candidate clicks an email invitation, they arrive at an interview details page that describes the role they are applying for and the number of questions to expect. They click “Start Interview,” at which point the VideoRecorder component loads and requests camera and microphone permission. Once permitted, the first question is displayed on screen and the candidate clicks a record button. The candidate records their response, stops recording, and previews the video. They can choose to re-record or click “Next” to move to the next question. After all questions are answered, the candidate sees a review page showing each recorded clip. Clicking “Submit” uploads all videos through the `/api/interviews/submit` endpoint, streaming them to cloud storage. A progress indicator shows upload status, and when complete, the candidate is redirected to a confirmation page before returning to their dashboard.

### Viewing AI Analysis Results
After a candidate submits their videos, the system enqueues a background job for processing. Once the AI services have analyzed facial expressions, voice sentiment, and gestures, the results are stored in the database. A recruiter viewing the Interviews list sees a status update indicating that results are ready. Clicking on a submitted candidate’s entry opens the Results page. This page features a video player synchronized with a timeline that highlights emotions, a searchable transcript generated by speech-to-text, and heatmaps showing gesture analysis. Recruiters can pause, rewind, and jump to any point in the video while the UI remains responsive.

## Settings and Account Management
Users access account settings by clicking “Settings” in the sidebar. The Settings page includes forms to update personal information such as name and email address. There is also an option to change the password, which asks for the current password and a new password before saving. Notification preferences are managed on the same page, enabling or disabling email alerts for new interview invitations or completed analysis results. After making changes, the user clicks “Save Changes,” sees a confirmation message, and remains on the Settings page. At any time, they can return to the dashboard by clicking the Dashboard link in the sidebar or header logo.

## Error States and Alternate Paths
If a user enters invalid credentials on sign-in, an inline error message appears under the form fields. A failed password reset due to an unrecognized email triggers a notification explaining that no account was found. During recording, if camera or microphone access is denied, a full-screen alert explains how to enable permissions in the browser settings. If a video upload fails because of a network interruption or file size limit, the app displays an error with a retry button. When a candidate tries to access an interview they are not invited to, or a recruiter tries to view submissions outside their organization, the application shows a 403 Forbidden page with guidance to return to the dashboard. Any nonexistent URL navigates to a 404 Not Found page, offering a link back to the home dashboard.

## Conclusion and Overall App Journey
From the first visit to everyday usage, RasproView AI guides users through a smooth and intuitive flow. New users sign up or accept an invitation, verify their email, and sign in. Recruiters create and send interview invitations, while candidates record responses question by question. The system handles uploads, processes videos with AI services in the background, and presents recruiters with rich analysis results. Users manage their profile and preferences in Settings, and the application gracefully handles any errors or permission issues along the way. This cohesive journey ensures that every user interaction is clear, connected, and focused on achieving the platform’s goal of seamless video-based interviewing and insightful AI-driven insights.
